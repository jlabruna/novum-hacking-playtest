# Test-only interpretations

Audio release v0.1.6 changes sound synthesis and cue selection only. It introduces no new gameplay assumptions; `test-1`, `streamlined-test-1` and `streamlined-test-2` retain their existing meanings, and historical runs are not recalculated.

Canonical mode implements NOVUM Network Intrusion as published in coordinated System Reference revision `2026-10-27-r4`. These choices are **playtest implementation assumptions**, not additions to tabletop canon. Canonical runs retain assumption-set identifier `test-1`. Historical v0.1.4 Streamlined runs retain `streamlined-test-1`. New v0.1.5 Streamlined runs use `streamlined-test-2`.

## Streamlined v0.1.5 — experimental, not canon (`streamlined-test-2`)

New Streamlined runs default to Breach 10 and full visibility, with an optional separately reported Breach 8 target. Alert 0–3 becomes starting Trace 0–3 directly; it changes no other roll or obstacle. Trace threshold is 8. Detection is permanent and starts Pursuit at 0; the triggering NET Action receives its normal end-of-action Pursuit advance. Basic Program Trace after detection does not turn into extra Pursuit. Cautious: TEST-ONLY check +2, success +2 Breach/+2 Trace, failure +2 Trace. Aggressive: check +0, success +3 Breach/+2 Trace, failure +3 Trace. Modifiers remain individually configurable and snapshotted at start. Three Memory replace specialist charges; each specialist costs one, Memory cannot recover mid-intrusion, and a new run resets it. Trace Removal may remove Trace due to Alert while undetected without changing Alert itself. Fortify's committed Memory remains spent after its one Pursuit block.

Successful movement first applies Basic Program Trace, then visits crossed positions in increasing order. At each position, resolve ICE, Resource Node and Sentry when present; the seeded generator normally places only one encounter per position. ICE wins or compatible Memory Bypass preserve movement; a loss stops at ICE and discards remaining movement, with +1 Trace before detection or +1 immediate Pursuit afterward. A future successful Basic Program check is required to retry. Draws repeat immediately; ICE's seeded choice is committed independently of the Hacker choice. Resource Nodes require a decision in crossing order. Skip permanently forfeits the node. Extract costs one extra NET Action, checks against network DV, and permanently grants or loses the node on success or failure. Override costs one Memory and guarantees extraction but still spends the NET Action. Any extraction adds +1 Trace before detection or +1 immediate Pursuit afterward, then performs the normal end-of-NET-Action Pursuit advance. Its action may cause ejection before remaining Basic movement resumes. Resolving a node otherwise preserves remaining movement. Resource rewards are separate optional objectives, subject to the scenario's declared primary objective.

Sentries are one-use: accepting reduces intended Breach advancement by one and adds +1 Trace while undetected or +1 immediate Pursuit when detected; Spoof spends one Memory and prevents both. A consumed Sentry cannot retrigger, even when movement is reduced. These penalties resolve before deciding whether the objective is complete. Detection and immediate encounter Pursuit happen once at their respective steps; finishing an ordinary NET Action advances Pursuit once. Objective completion wins a same-action catch only after all applicable encounter movement penalties. Two NET Actions cost one physical Main Action. Pending ICE, Sentry and node decisions, the remaining movement cursor and the seed-based ICE state persist with the saved active run. Returning to the same run cannot restore Memory.

Canonical and v0.1.4 Streamlined retain their own engine paths, thresholds and historical records. This app does not simulate external facility incidents, objective declarations, re-entry after disconnect or physical patrols. The simulator has explicit strategic policies; its results are observations, not balance changes or canonical percentages.

- The scenario establishes the initial network connection. The app does not model re-entry, reconnection, an enemy Hacker, or combat elsewhere.
- When Breach reaches 10 on the same NET Action as a possible Pursuit catch, objective completion wins. Pursuit still advances on the action that first reaches Trace 6, unless Breach 10 has completed.
- Fortify occupies the segment immediately behind the Hacker when invoked, provided that segment is ahead of Pursuit and greater than zero. One arrival of Pursuit at that segment is prevented, consuming the position. A later Fortify replaces an earlier one.
- If one successful advancement crosses multiple Resource Nodes, the player may stop at the earliest and discard the rest of the advancement, or pass all crossed nodes and continue. Later nodes remain available on a future action only if the player stopped before reaching them.
- Nodes represent one extracted playtest reward each. No NOVUM credit value, charge restoration, or lasting equipment benefit is assigned. Remaining active nodes at the end are counted as left uncollected.
- The generator assigns one element per segment among positions 1–9. This prevents collisions and leaves the objective at 10. It does not bias encounter placement by difficulty or tier.
- ICE responses draw from a deterministic sequence derived from the network seed and are revealed only after each player choice. Digital d20 rolls use local browser randomness.

Questions about Fortify, simultaneous outcomes, node crossing, reconnection, and specialist ownership remain for Planning. Edit the assumption-set identifier when changing these interpretations, and retain old run metadata for analysis.

## Streamlined Intrusion — experimental, not canon (`streamlined-test-1`)

The v0.1.4 app offers a separate Streamlined ruleset for comparing flow and outcome with Canonical mode. It has no Barriers or Tunnel and may target Breach 8 or 10. A successful Cautious or Aggressive action applies its normal Trace, then resolves crossed positions in order. ICE at a crossed position interrupts the same NET Action: victory or compatible one-charge Bypass resumes the remaining movement; defeat stops at that ICE and loses the rest. Draws immediately replay without extra action or Trace. A new Basic Program check is required to retry active ICE; failure gives no free confrontation. Multiple ICE resolve in position order. Resource Nodes and Sentries keep their existing decisions and consequences within the same movement action. End-of-action Pursuit advances only once; immediate penalties still apply. The existing test-only objective-before-catch precedence remains.

Streamlined Cautious defaults to a TEST-ONLY +2 check modifier; Aggressive defaults +0. Both remain editable independently of Canonical's +0/+0 defaults. Neither alters Breach, Trace, Pursuit, specialist charge costs, natural-roll overrides, or the network-tier DV. Both modes retain three shared specialist charges. The mode, objective and modifiers are recorded at run creation; a later setup change cannot rewrite them. Historical runs without a mode or objective are interpreted as Canonical/Breach 10. Generator placements remain unique and within positions 1 through one less than the selected objective. No claim is made that the experimental rules are approved for NOVUM tabletop canon.
